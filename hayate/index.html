Hayate

One shortcut, and your app is in front.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

Hayate is a small menu bar app that gives each of your apps its own keyboard shortcut. Press it from anywhere to open the app, or bring it to the front if it’s already running. No more hunting through the Dock or cycling with ⌘Tab.

Open

  • Assign a shortcut such as ⌃⌥S or ⌥⌘1 to any app
  • Opens the app if it isn’t running, and brings it to the front if it is — restoring minimized windows like a click on its Dock icon
  • Suggestions for apps you use often make setup quick

Switch

  • Press the shortcut again while the app is in front to switch to its next window (optional)
  • Pause all shortcuts from the menu bar when you need the keys for something else

Keep in sync

  • Sync your shortcuts with other Macs through iCloud (optional)
  • Export and import your shortcuts as a file
  • Free for up to 3 shortcuts; Hayate Pro, a one-time purchase, raises the limit to 100

Requires macOS 15 or later.
