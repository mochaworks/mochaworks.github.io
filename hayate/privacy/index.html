Privacy Policy

Hayate · Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Hayate for Mac (“the app”) handles your information. In short: the app does not collect any data, and your shortcuts stay on your Mac unless you turn on iCloud sync, in which case they are stored in your own iCloud.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share personal information. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs, and the developer runs no server for it. It is not given network access of its own under the macOS App Sandbox.

Your shortcuts and settings

  • The list of shortcuts you register (the app’s name, its bundle identifier, and the key combination) and your settings are stored only on your Mac.
  • Hayate responds only to the key combinations you register, through the shortcut mechanism macOS provides. It does not monitor, record, or store any other keystrokes.

iCloud sync (optional)

iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, your shortcut list is stored in your iCloud account through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so that your other Macs signed in to the same Apple Account can use it. This data is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; the developer cannot access it. Turning sync off stops syncing.

Suggested apps

To suggest apps you may want a shortcut for, the app looks up apps you have used in the past 7 days (using Spotlight on your Mac) and apps that are currently running. This happens only on your Mac, only while the suggestions are shown, and the results are neither saved nor sent anywhere.

Accessibility permission (optional)

The Window Cycling option switches to the next window of the app that is in front. To do this, macOS requires you to allow Hayate under Accessibility. The app uses this permission only to list that app’s windows and bring the next one forward when you press its shortcut. It does not read window contents, what you type, or anything else, and it is not used for any other purpose. The app works without this permission; only Window Cycling needs it. You can turn it off at any time in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.

Export and import

When you export, the app writes your shortcut list to a file at the location you choose. When you import, it reads only the file you choose. The app does not send these files anywhere.

Hayate Pro (in-app purchase)

Hayate Pro is an optional one-time purchase. Prices are shown and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, under Apple’s privacy policy. The app only asks the App Store whether Hayate Pro has been purchased on your Apple Account. It does not receive your name, email address, Apple Account, or payment details. The developer sees only the sales reports Apple provides, which do not identify individual buyers.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact support@moshisora.com.
