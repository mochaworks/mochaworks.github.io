Hayate Support

Help with setting up shortcuts, syncing, and Hayate Pro.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email support@moshisora.com. Including your macOS version, the app and shortcut involved, and the steps that led to the problem helps a lot.

Getting started

  1. Click the Hayate icon in the menu bar and choose Edit Shortcuts….
  2. Click Add App… and choose an app, or click one of the Suggested apps.
  3. Click Record Shortcut and press the key combination you want, such as ⌃⌥S.
  4. Press the shortcut from any app. Hayate opens the app, or brings it to the front if it is already running.

Which keys can I use?

  • Hold at least one of ⌃ Control, ⌥ Option, or ⌘ Command. ⇧ Shift can be added.
  • Then press a letter, number, or symbol key (A–Z, 0–9, - = [ ] ; ' , . / and so on).
  • Function keys, arrow keys, Space, Return, Tab, and Delete cannot be used.

Frequently asked questions

A shortcut doesn’t work.

  • Check that shortcuts aren’t paused. A crossed-out bolt in the menu bar means they are; turn off Pause Shortcuts in the menu.
  • Another app may already use the same key combination. Try a different one.
  • In the free version, only the first 3 shortcuts in the list are active. Others are marked “Inactive: over the free version limit.” Move a shortcut up, or get Hayate Pro.
  • Shortcuts work only while Hayate is running. To start it automatically, turn on Launch at login in Settings.

Pressing the shortcut again does nothing when the app is already in front.

That is the default. To switch to the app’s next window instead, turn on Window Cycling in Settings (see below).

How do I set up Window Cycling? Why does it need Accessibility?

Open Settings and turn on Switch windows when the app is already in front. macOS then asks you to allow Hayate under Accessibility: open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility and turn on Hayate. macOS requires this permission for one app to bring another app’s window to the front. Hayate uses it only for this, and everything else works without it. Minimized windows and windows on other desktops are skipped.

If Window Cycling still doesn’t work after you allowed it, remove Hayate from the Accessibility list with the “−” button, then turn the option off and on again in Hayate’s Settings to add it back.

How does iCloud sync work?

Turn on Sync shortcuts with iCloud in Settings on each Mac. Your shortcuts sync between Macs signed in to the same Apple Account. A shortcut for an app that isn’t installed on a Mac stays turned off on that Mac until the app is installed. Syncing may take a little while, and requires iCloud to be on in System Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

How do I move my shortcuts to another Mac without iCloud?

In Settings, under Export and Import, click Export… to save a file. On the other Mac, click Import… and choose the file. Merge adds the shortcuts to your current list; Replace discards your current list.

What does Hayate Pro include?

The free version lets you register up to 3 shortcuts. Hayate Pro is a one-time purchase that raises the limit to 100. There is no subscription. Buy it from Settings > Hayate Pro.

How do I restore my purchase on a new Mac?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought Hayate Pro with, then open Hayate’s Settings and click Restore Purchases. If you see “No previous purchase was found,” check that you are signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I redeem an offer code?

Open the redemption link that came with the code, or open the App Store app, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, choose Redeem Gift Card, and enter the code. Then open Hayate’s Settings. If Hayate Pro is not shown as active, click Restore Purchases.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose “Request a refund.” Apple makes the decision.

Does Hayate collect my data or watch what I type?

No. Hayate only responds to the shortcuts you register and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.

Hayate requires macOS 15 or later.
