KoKo Markup Support

Help with capturing, annotating, and sharing.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email support@moshisora.com. Including your macOS version and the steps that led to the problem helps a lot.

Getting started

  1. Click Capture Screen in the window, or press ⌃⇧⌘2 from any app, and drag to select an area.
  2. Pick a tool from the toolbar and drag on the image to draw.
  3. Drag the tab at the bottom of the window into a chat, email, or document — or copy the image with ⇧⌘C.

Keyboard shortcuts

Capture (works from any app)

  • ⌃⇧⌘2 Capture a selection
  • ⌃⇧⌘1 Capture the full screen
  • ⌃⇧⌘R Capture the previous selection again

Tools

  • V Select · A Arrow · R Rectangle · E Ellipse · L Line · P Freehand
  • H Highlight · M Mosaic · T Text · N Number · C Crop

Editing and sharing

  • ⌘Z Undo · ⇧⌘Z Redo · ⌘⌫ Delete the selected annotation
  • ⌘V Paste an image · ⇧⌘C Copy the image · ⌘S Export

Frequently asked questions

Capturing shows only the desktop, or nothing happens.

The app needs Screen Recording permission. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on KoKo Markup, then quit and reopen the app.

A capture shortcut doesn’t work.

Another app may already be using the same key combination. You can still capture from the menu bar icon or the Screenshot menu. Try quitting other screenshot or window-management apps to find the conflict.

The pasted image looks twice as large.

Screenshots on a Retina display have twice as many pixels as what you see. Choose 50% from the size menu at the bottom right of the window to export at the size shown on screen.

How do I hide personal information?

Choose the Mosaic tool (M) and drag over the area. You can switch between pixelate and a solid color from the toolbar.

Does KoKo Markup upload my screenshots?

No. Your images never leave your Mac, and the app collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.

Is KoKo Markup free? What are the tips in Settings?

Every feature is free, with no ads. If you enjoy the app, you can leave a one-time tip from KoKo Markup > Settings… > Support. Tips are entirely optional and don’t unlock anything — the app works the same whether or not you tip. You can tip more than once.

Can I get a refund for a tip?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose “Request a refund.” Apple makes the decision.

KoKo Markup requires macOS 15 or later.
