Privacy Policy

KoKo Markup · Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how KoKo Markup for Mac (“the app”) handles your information. In short: the app does not collect any data, and your images never leave your Mac.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share personal information. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

Your screenshots and images

  • Screenshots you capture and images you open, paste, or drop are processed only on your Mac.
  • The app does not connect to the internet. It is not given network access under the macOS App Sandbox, so it cannot send your images anywhere.
  • Images are saved or shared only when you choose to — by dragging, copying, saving, or using the Share menu. Where they go after that is up to you and the app or service you choose.
  • When you use the Share menu, the app writes a temporary copy of the image to its own sandboxed folder so the destination can read it. These copies are deleted automatically after about an hour.

Screen Recording permission

To capture the screen, macOS asks you to allow Screen Recording for the app. The app uses this permission only at the moment you start a capture. You can turn it off at any time in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording.

Settings

Your preferences, such as the last color and line width you used, are stored only on your Mac.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact support@moshisora.com.
