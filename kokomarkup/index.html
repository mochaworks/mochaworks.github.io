KoKo Markup

Capture, mark up, and share in seconds.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

KoKo Markup is a simple annotation app for quickly marking up screenshots and handing them off. Reporting a bug, explaining a setting, or giving design feedback — point out what matters and send it with as few steps as possible.

Capture

  • Capture a selected area, a window, or the full screen
  • Global shortcuts that work from any app: ⌃⇧⌘2 selection, ⌃⇧⌘1 full screen, ⌃⇧⌘R previous selection
  • Or bring in an image by pasting, dropping, or opening a file

Mark up

  • Arrows, rectangles, ellipses, lines, freehand, highlight, text, and step numbers
  • Mosaic to hide sensitive information
  • Crop, and move or resize anything after you draw it

Share

  • Drag the image straight into a chat, email, or document
  • Copy, save as PNG or JPEG, or share via AirDrop, Messages, or Mail

Requires macOS 15 or later.
